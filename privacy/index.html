Privacy Policy

Bulk URL Removal for Search Console — Chrome extension

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Your URL lists never leave your browser. This extension automates clicks on a Search Console page you are already signed in to. It has no server, no account, and no analytics. The only data that ever leaves your computer is a license key, and only if you choose to buy the paid version.

What the extension does

Google Search Console accepts URL removal requests one at a time. This extension fills in and submits that form repeatedly for a list of URLs you provide, inside your own browser, on your own verified property. It does not use any Google API and has no access to your Google account beyond the page you have open.

About the license check

If you purchase the unlimited version, you receive a license key. When you paste that key into the extension, the key and a fixed label (chrome-extension) are sent to Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider, so it can confirm the key is genuine. The check repeats at most once a week. Nothing else is included in that request — not your URLs, not your Search Console property, not your Google account.

Payment itself happens on Lemon Squeezy's own checkout page, not inside this extension. Your card details are never seen by the extension or by its developer.

What this extension never does

  • It does not collect, transmit, or store your URLs, sitemaps, or Search Console data.
  • It does not read your Google account, email, or any other Google service.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, or fingerprinting.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share data with anyone. There is nothing to sell.
  • It does not run on any site other than Search Console.

No other hosts are contacted, and no broad site access is requested.

Deleting your data

Everything the extension stores lives in your browser. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all of it. You can also clear the saved job and license at any time from the extension's own panel.

Children

This is a tool for website owners and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what data is handled, the change will be described here and the date above will be updated.

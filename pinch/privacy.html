Privacy Policy

Pinch for macOS

Pinch collects nothing. It has no network access, so there is nowhere for your data to go.

What is collected

Nothing. Pinch does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or any part of your images. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Why that claim is verifiable

Pinch is distributed as a sandboxed Mac App Store application, and it is built without the com.apple.security.network.client entitlement. macOS enforces this at the system level: the app is structurally unable to open an outbound network connection, whether or not the developer intended one. This is not a promise about future behavior — it is a property of the shipped binary, and Apple verifies it during review.

Your images

Images you add to Pinch are read and written entirely on your Mac. Compressed output is written only to the folder you choose, or over the original file when you explicitly enable Replace Originals. Nothing is copied, cached, or retained elsewhere.

Pinch can access a file only after you give it that file — by dropping it on the window, choosing it in the open panel, or selecting an output folder. This is enforced by the macOS App Sandbox; the app cannot browse your disk on its own.

Image metadata

Photographs often carry EXIF metadata, which can include GPS coordinates. Pinch reads this data only to decide what to copy into the output file, according to the setting you pick: keep everything, remove location only, or remove all metadata. It is never transmitted, and it is never stored anywhere outside the output image itself.

Purchases

Pinch is a one-time purchase through the Mac App Store. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the developer receives no payment details and no personally identifying information about purchasers.

Children

Pinch is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date below. Any change that would introduce data collection would also require adding network access to the app, which would be visible in the App Store release notes.
